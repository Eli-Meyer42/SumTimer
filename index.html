    Quick start

    1. Tap 🎤 Listen and allow the microphone.
    2. In champ select, say each enemy’s spells: “top has flash teleport”, or one breath: “lineup top flash teleport jungle flash smite …”.
    3. When the game clock shows 0:00, say “game start”. Or say the time you see: “time 0 15”. This starts a new game.
    4. When an enemy uses a spell, say it: “mid flash”.
    5. You’ll hear a call at 30 seconds and when it’s back.

    Voice commands

    If you call a spell before the clock is set, the timer starts and it asks “What minute is the game at?”. Answer with just the number: “3”, “3 minutes” or “3 30”. Objectives wait for your answer, then get logged. Words with no commands in them are logged in gray, not red.

    Anything longer than 6 words is ignored unless it starts with a command word (a role, a spell, time, clock, note, bug, lineup, dragon …), so normal conversation won’t set things off. The clock only changes when you start with “time”, “game time”, “game timer”, “timer”, “clock” or “set timer to”.

Calling a spell that’s already running also just tells you its time left. Say “top flash again” (or “restart top flash”) to start it over.

    History tab

    • Pick another game from the dropdown. Every button tap is recorded too, so you can see what you had to click.

    Flash check (optional)

    • Off by default. Turn it on in Edit with the Flash check button, or say “flash reminders on” (“flash reminders off” to stop).
    • Every 60 seconds it says which enemies are missing Flash and how long until it’s back: “No Flash: Support 1:20, Jungle 3:05”. It stays quiet when everyone has Flash.

    Spells before the game

    • Set enemy spells any time before “game start” (“lineup …” or “mid has flash ignite”). A dashed Ready box on the Timers tab shows what’s set.
    • They carry into the next game. They’re only cleared when you say “clear spells” (or “reset spells”), or when the next game starts more than 15 minutes after the last one ended.

    Notes

    • Long notes: once you say “note” (or “bug”), everything you say is part of the note until you pause for 3 seconds or say “end note”. Then it’s saved as one note. Commands aren’t run while a note is recording.
    • “save that as a note”, “make that a note” or “I meant that as a note” saves your previous message as a note, even if it was ignored as conversation.
    • App notes: start with “bug” (or “feedback”, “issue”, “app note”): “bug it said dragon up twice”. It saves the note plus the last 4 things the app heard or did, so Claude can see what went wrong. Shown with 🐞 in the Log. Filter with “Only app notes”, or tap Copy app notes (all games) and paste them to Claude.
    • Start with “note”: “note I keep dying to their jungler bot side”. Everything after “note” is saved word for word and nothing else happens, even if it mentions Flash or dragon.
    • Or say “note”, wait for the buzz, then start talking within 10 seconds.
    • Start with “good” or “liked” to tag it 👍, or “bad”, “didn’t like” or “mistake” to tag it 👎.
    • You hear a short “Note saved”, get a buzz, and see “Note saved” on screen.
    • Notes show in green in the Log with their game time. Filter with “Only notes”. They’re included in Copy and Export.

    Alerts

    • A call at 30 seconds (“Mid Flash in 30”) and when it’s back (“Mid Flash up”). Teleport warns 1:30 early instead (“Top Teleport in a minute and a half”).
    • When you call a spell, it reads back when it’s up: “Mid f 21:32” (or time left if the clock isn’t running).
    • Spells coming back together are merged into one call: “Mid and Bot Flash up”.
    • The strip under the text box shows the next 4 spells due back. Under 30 seconds is highlighted.
    • The red badge shows how many enemy Flashes are down.

    Team chat

    • With the clock running, starting a timer or asking for status shows a chat line like mid f 14:32.
    • When an enemy Flash comes back, it shows mid f up.
    • Type the line into League chat. Tap the box to hide it.

    Settings

    • Voice on/off: spoken alerts. Chimes still play when off.
    • Voice picker: choose a voice. Picking one plays a sample.
    • 🎤 Listen: always-on listening. Pauses while the app speaks, then resumes.
    • New game: starts by voice with “game start”, or on its own when you set an early clock time after a game.
    • On PC: there’s no 50-minute “Keep recording?” prompt, and a game with the clock running isn’t ended for no input before 60:00. Games end at 60:00 (or 30 minutes after your last command if the clock isn’t running).
    • Status light (left of the clock): green = listening, yellow = restarting, red = mic off. Tap it to start or restart the mic. A red Voice output stalled banner appears if spoken replies stop; tap it to test the voice.
    • 50-minute check (phone): at 50:00 it asks “Keep recording?” Say “yes” (or tap Keep recording) to continue, and it asks again every 10 minutes. Say “stop”, or don’t answer within 1 minute, and it stops listening, stops the clock and clears the timers.

    Log

    The Log tab shows every command you said, with game time, real time, and what the app did. Anything it didn’t understand is red. Alerts and taps show in gray. Pick a game from the dropdown (a new game starts when you set the clock at the start of a game, or say “game start”). Filter to only what you said or only what wasn’t understood. Tap Copy this game and paste it to Claude to fix problems. It keeps the last 60 games. To see one device’s games on another, tap Export all (or Export this game for just the game picked in the dropdown), email the file to yourself, then tap Import on the other device and pick the file. Import merges without duplicates, so do it in both directions to keep phone and PC matched.

    Troubleshooting

    • Mic permission keeps popping up: you opened the file directly from your computer. Browsers don’t remember mic permission for files. Open your Netlify link instead and allow it once.
    • One tab at a time: if Sum Timer is open in two tabs, only one listens and runs timers. The other shows Running in another tab with a button to switch.
    • Background tab: timers keep running on time even when League has focus. Only if the device really sleeps (no ticks for 60+ seconds) are late alerts skipped and logged on their own row as “Missed while asleep”.
    • Voice on PC: each line plays in order. If Chrome’s voice stalls, the app retries once and logs “Voice stalled”.
    • Mic after replies: after the app speaks, listening restarts on its own. If the mic ever stops while Listening is on, it’s restarted within a few seconds. The self-test checks that the mic is listening again before it says “All good”.
    • Log tab: taps are hidden by default. Tick Show taps to see them. Copies and exports always include taps and everything that was ignored as conversation.
    • Spoken replies: every command that changes something gets a short spoken reply, for example “Support Cosmic on”.
    • Mic dropped: after a network or mic error the app retries on its own (1s, 2s, 5s, 10s, then every 30s). After 10 failed tries a red Mic off — tap to restart banner appears. Repeated errors are logged once with a count (“Mic error: network ×12”), and error rows never push your commands out of the log.
    • Quests and clock jumps: quests are assumed at 14:00 when the clock gets there, or when you set it past 14:00. A sudden jump of more than 3 minutes past 14:00 (likely a mishearing) doesn’t assume them. Say “mid quest” if they did finish.
    • Games end on their own after 30 minutes with no input, or at 60:00 if you never said “yes” to keep recording. The next thing you say or tap starts a fresh game.
    • Changing the clock: every timer keeps its “back at” game time, so time left moves with the clock. Jump the clock ahead a minute and every timer has a minute less; jump past a timer and it’s up. Great for skipping around in replays.
    • Alerts while the app is in the background are played when you come back if they’re under 10 seconds old, otherwise skipped.
    • “Mic blocked”: the Claude app can’t use the mic. Use your Netlify link in Chrome.
    • No sound in earphones: make sure Voice is on, and that dictation isn’t using your earphones’ mic.
    • A command was misheard: check the line under the clock, then copy the log and send it to Claude.
